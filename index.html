Buyer's Guide

Material Sustainability


When choosing a sustainable yoga mat, look for natural materials like rubber or jute. Mats made from recycled materials also contribute to sustainability while providing durability.

Grip Performance


Grip is crucial for hot yoga, as sweat can make surfaces slippery. Opt for mats with textured surfaces or those made from rubber for optimal grip.

Size and Thickness


Most hot yoga mats range from 4mm to 6mm thick. Thicker mats provide more cushioning, while thinner options offer better stability. Choose according to your comfort preference and practice style.

Eco-Friendly Certifications


Look for mats that are OEKO-TEX certified or made from biodegradable materials. These certifications ensure that the mats are free from harmful chemicals and safe for the environment.

FAQs

What materials are best for sustainable yoga mats?


Natural rubber, jute, cork, and recycled materials are the best options for sustainable yoga mats. These materials are eco-friendly and provide good durability.

How do I clean my eco-friendly yoga mat?


Most eco-friendly mats can be cleaned with a mixture of water and mild soap. Avoid harsh chemicals that can degrade the materials and shorten the mat's lifespan.

Are cork yoga mats better for hot yoga?


Yes, cork yoga mats offer excellent grip, especially when wet, making them ideal for hot yoga. They also have natural antimicrobial properties, which can help maintain cleanliness.

What thickness is ideal for hot yoga mats?


A thickness of 4mm to 6mm is ideal for hot yoga mats. Thicker mats provide cushioning, while thinner options offer stability, so choose based on your personal preference.

How do I choose the right yoga mat for my practice?


Consider the type of yoga you practice, your grip needs, and your environmental values. Test mats if possible to find what feels best for you in terms of comfort and performance.
